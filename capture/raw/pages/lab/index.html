Thirty sketches, none of them the home page

Every idea here is isolated on its own page, with the knobs exposed, so it can be judged by turning it rather than by reading about it. Nothing in this directory is imported by the site — the sketches share the meadow’s vocabulary and none of its page.

The dots are effort: one is an afternoon, three has unknowns in it. A line in red means the hook is already written and sitting unused.
